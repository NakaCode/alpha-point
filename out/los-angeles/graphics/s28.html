DUMPING THE ROCK. Automatic Barge Fails to Do Just What Was Expected. The operation of dumping a load of rock and thereby actually commencing the harbor work was not so successful as it had been hoped that it would be, but the result furnished valuable information for later construction on the sea wall. […] Probably fifty tons of rock lying near the upper edge were dropped off by hand and with bars.
