  campaigned for annexation." vem antes da contagem, como `VOZ-ANTI-IA.md` §3 pede, e "So the strip is a legal handshake, drawn
  half a mile wide, so that one city could touch two others. A handshake is not a merger. That took almost three more years."
  responde sem dizer que respondeu, e passa o bastão a 1909.
